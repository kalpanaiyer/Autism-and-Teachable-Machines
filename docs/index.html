Pre- and Post-Study Questionnaires

  1. What is Artificial intelligence (we will call it AI from now on)?
    1. Putting your intelligence into Computer
    2. Programming with your own intelligence
    3. Making a Machine intelligent
    4. Putting more memory into Computer

    Answer: (c)
    Reason: Because AI is to make things work automatically through machine without using human effort. Machine will give the result with just giving input from human. Tha tmeans the system or machine will act as per the requirement.

  2. Does a rock have AI? (Yes/No)
  3. Does my pen have AI? (Yes/No)
  4. Does a dog have AI? (Yes/No)
  5. Does your math teacher have AI? (Yes/No)
  6. Does [Google maps/other applications name depending on the child age] have AI? (Yes/No)
  7. Does your phone have AI? (Yes/No)
  8. Ways that computers are better than humans (choose all that apply). Computers (apps, machines, robots, etc.):

    • are better for communication

    • are better at catching a something
    • are better at seeing
    • are better at being original
    • are better at improvising
    • are better at driving
    • are better at hearing
    • are better at remembering things
    • are better at tasting
    • don't need to sleep or eat
    • are better at touching

  9. How do computers (apps, machines, or robots) become intelligent?
  10. How would you train a computer to smile?

  12. What would be very difficult for a computer to learn? Why?
